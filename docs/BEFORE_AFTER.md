# Before and after

## Before

An analyst who wants to understand a company opens its yearly and quarterly reports by hand. Each one runs to a hundred pages or more. They copy figures into notes, check them against last year's report, and write a summary for the team. One company can take an afternoon, and details slip through.

## After

1. **Gather.** You type a company name. The assistant pulls its latest reports from the official public record.
2. **Check.** It confirms the company is one it covers and that the reports arrived complete. If not, it says so plainly.
3. **Summarize.** It answers your question in a few sentences, using only what the reports say.
4. **Show.** Every answer names the report it came from, so you can check it yourself.

An afternoon becomes a few minutes.
